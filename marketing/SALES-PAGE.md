# SourceGlo Pro
### Production Intelligence for Better Mixes

**Your mix is only as good as what you feed it.** SourceGlo Pro tells you —
in one number — whether that kick, 808, snare or vocal is ready, what exactly
is holding it back, and then fixes it. And when a sample is beyond saving, it
hands you a better one from your own library.

---

## Know before you build

Drop SourceGlo Pro on the track, pick the source type, hit **ANALYZE** while
it plays. Seconds later:

- **Source Score 0–100** against a modern pro standard — READY, GOOD, NEEDS
  WORK, or FIX REQUIRED.
- **Five pods** break it down: Tone, Punch, Level, Phase, Fit.
- **Diagnostics that name the problem**: kick/808 masking with the exact
  frequency range, headroom running hot, weak low-end body, mud, harshness,
  phase cancellation, wide bass that will fold up in mono. Clean sources get
  told they're clean.
- **Tempo and key detection** built in — the 808's root *is* the key, and
  SourceGlo knows it.

## One gold button

**FIX SOURCE** applies the correction the analysis computed: counter-EQ
toward the target for that source type, headroom pulled to a −1 dBTP
ceiling, bass summed to mono below 120 Hz when it needs it. **Fix Amount**
scales the whole correction from a nudge to the full fix, and **A/B**
flips you back to the raw source any time. No clicks, ever.

## Eight macros that mean it

Punch and Transients shape attacks (the thump and the crack, separately).
Body, Tone and Air place the balance. Saturate drives an oversampled tanh
stage — up to 8× — and Stereo widens without ever touching your mono core.

## Rescue: the honest feature

Some samples can't be saved — and SourceGlo Pro says so. The rescue panel
ranks **your own sample library** against what the source was supposed to
be: preview a candidate in place, star your favourites, and **drag the row
straight into your project**. Point it at your packs once; it works in every
session after.

## Under the hood, for those who ask

- BS.1770-style 4× true-peak metering
- High-resolution chroma key detection, bass-weighted for 808 culture
- Onset-autocorrelation tempo detection, 60–180 BPM
- Analysis on a worker thread; the audio thread never blocks, never
  allocates, never locks
- 29 factory presets by source type; your presets never touch your gain
  staging
- Full-fat resizable UI, 70–150 %, crisp at every step
