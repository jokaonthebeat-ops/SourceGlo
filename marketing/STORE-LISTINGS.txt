=== SHORT (one line, <=140 chars) ===
SourceGlo Pro scores your source 0-100, names what's wrong, fixes it - and
when it can't be saved, finds a better sample in YOUR library.

=== MEDIUM (store card, ~60 words) ===
Production intelligence for better mixes. SourceGlo Pro analyzes your kick,
808, snare or vocal, scores it against a modern pro standard, and shows
exactly what's wrong - masking, headroom, weak body, phase. One gold button
fixes it. Eight macros finish it. And the rescue engine ranks your own
sample library for a better candidate, draggable straight into your project.
VST3/AU/Standalone, macOS universal.

=== LONG (product page) ===
Use marketing/SALES-PAGE.md.

=== FEATURE BULLETS ===
- Source Score 0-100 with Tone / Punch / Level / Phase / Fit pods
- Plain-language diagnostics with exact frequency ranges
- Tempo + key detection (bass-weighted chroma - it knows 808 culture)
- FIX SOURCE: analysis-derived counter-EQ, headroom trim, low-end mono repair
- Fix Amount scaling and click-free A/B against the raw source
- 8 macros: Punch, Body, Tone, Air, Stereo, Transients, Saturate, Output
- Oversampled saturation (2x/4x/8x) with correct host latency reporting
- Rescue engine: ranks YOUR sample library, previews in place, drags into
  your DAW
- 29 factory presets by source type + your own, with modified indicator
- BS.1770-style true-peak metering
- VST3 + AU + Standalone, macOS 11+ universal; resizable 70-150%

=== REQUIREMENTS ===
macOS 11 Big Sur or later, Intel or Apple Silicon.
VST3 host, AU host (Logic/GarageBand), or standalone.
