INSTALL
-------

Open "Install SourceGlo Pro.pkg" and follow the steps. You can choose which
formats to install:

  VST3         /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
               Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Reaper, Studio One, Bitwig

  Audio Unit   /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
               Logic Pro and GarageBand

  Standalone   /Applications
               Check a sample without opening a DAW

If the plugin does not show up, trigger a plugin rescan in your DAW. Logic
rescans Audio Units on launch.


WHAT IT DOES
------------

SourceGlo Pro qualifies your source before you build on it. Drop it on a
kick, 808, snare or vocal, pick the source type, and press ANALYZE while it
plays:

  SOURCE SCORE   0-100 against a modern pro standard, with five pods:
                 Tone, Punch, Level, Phase and Fit
  DIAGNOSTICS    what is actually wrong - masking, headroom, weak body,
                 mud, harshness, phase - and what is already clean
  FIX SOURCE     applies the correction the analysis computed: counter-EQ,
                 headroom trim, low-end mono repair. Fix Amount scales it,
                 A/B compares against the raw source
  MACROS         Punch, Body, Tone, Air, Stereo, Transients, Saturate and
                 Output - the finishing moves, oversampled where it counts
  RESCUE         when the source is beyond saving, ranked replacements from
                 YOUR OWN sample library. Preview in place, drag the row
                 straight into your project


THE WORKFLOW
------------

  1. Insert SourceGlo Pro on the source track.
  2. Pick the source type (or leave Auto), play the loop, press ANALYZE.
  3. Read the score and the diagnostics. The FIT tab shows the band
     balance against the target; DETAIL shows every measurement.
  4. FIX SOURCE to correct it - or check the rescue list and drag in a
     better sample.
  5. Shape with the macros, save your chain as a preset (SAVE AS).

Add your sample folders once in the LIBRARY tab and the rescue suggestions
work in every session.


TIPS
----

  - Analysis measures the SOURCE (before the fix), so the score tells you
    what you are working with. When you press FIX SOURCE the spectrum
    flips to Post so you can see the correction working; A/B it to hear it.
  - While the fix is engaged the gold button reads FIX - 50%. Drag up or
    down on it to ride the amount from a nudge to the full correction.
  - Presets cover the creative controls only. Your gain staging, routing
    and quality settings never jump when you switch presets.
  - Cmd+Z undoes a preset switch.
