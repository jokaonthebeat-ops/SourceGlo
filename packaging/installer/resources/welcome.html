SourceGlo Pro

Production intelligence for better mixes: analyzes your source, scores it against a modern pro standard, pinpoints problems, and lines up fixes and rescue suggestions.

Preview build 0.9.1 — the full interface with the analysis engine still in development. Scores, diagnostics and rescue suggestions currently show demonstration data.

This installer places

  • VST3 in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • Standalone app in /Applications

You can choose which of the three to install on the next screens. Installing to the system folders requires your administrator password.

Requires

  • macOS 11 Big Sur or later
  • Apple Silicon or Intel — this is a universal build
