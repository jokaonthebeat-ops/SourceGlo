Installed

SourceGlo Pro is ready to use.

Finding it in your DAW

  • Most hosts pick up a new plug-in on their next launch. If SourceGlo Pro does not appear, run your DAW's plug-in rescan.
  • Logic Pro validates new Audio Units on launch; allow it to finish before opening a project.

Where to start

Drop it on a kick, 808 or vocal, pick the source type in the left panel and hit ANALYZE while it plays. The score ring, diagnostics and Fit view show what the source needs; FIX SOURCE applies the correction, and A/B compares. Add your sample folders in the Library tab so the rescue suggestions can pull from your own packs.

Presets live in the header — click the name to browse, and SAVE AS keeps your own starting points.
